Privacy reference

Privacy questions for guest-created event media

Event albums combine personal media, a temporary audience, and changing access expectations. Organizers should define the collection purpose and visibility model before displaying a QR code, rather than deciding after guests have begun contributing.

Questions to answer in advance

  • Who may upload, and who may view the resulting collection?
  • Are contributions visible immediately or only after moderation?
  • How long will original files and contribution metadata be retained?
  • How can a guest request removal of a contribution?
  • Which vendors process storage, delivery, or moderation data?

Practical communication

The upload page should identify the event context, explain what will happen to a contribution, and link to the applicable privacy information. Public event walls require a different disclosure from a private host-only collection. Organizers should avoid copying media into additional tools without checking whether that new use matches the original expectation.

Retention should be deliberate. Export deadlines, read-only transitions, and deletion workflows are easier to manage when they are part of the event plan. This page is an operational checklist, not legal advice; requirements depend on the organizer, jurisdiction, audience, and use case.
