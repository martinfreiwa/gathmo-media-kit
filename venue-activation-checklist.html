Venue checklist

Activating guest media collection at a venue

A venue can make guest media collection more reliable by treating it as an event operation rather than a decorative QR sign. The workflow should have an owner, a tested destination, and a fallback plan before doors open.

Pre-event check

  • Confirm the correct album and event date with the organizer.
  • Scan every printed code from a guest network, not only staff Wi-Fi.
  • Test image and short video uploads from at least two mobile platforms.
  • Agree who moderates contributions and who may change display settings.
  • Place a short fallback URL beside each code.

During the event

Monitor the first real uploads, check that approved items reach the intended display, and keep contribution independent from live-wall playback. If venue connectivity degrades, guests should receive a retryable message rather than a false success state.

Handover

At the end of the booking, confirm the host's administrative access, export expectations, post-event upload window, and removal route. Document any display device or network configuration that should not persist into the next event.
