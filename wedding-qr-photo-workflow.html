Wedding workflow

Planning an app-free wedding QR photo workflow

A wedding QR code succeeds when guests understand the invitation, reach the upload page quickly, and see a clear completion signal. The code itself is only one part of the guest journey; placement, wording, network conditions, and host follow-up determine participation.

Before the wedding

  • Create one canonical album destination and test it on iOS and Android.
  • Print a short human-readable URL next to the QR code as a fallback.
  • Use plain wording such as “Share your photos and messages here.”
  • Run a rehearsal with several simultaneous photo and video uploads.

During the celebration

Place the invitation where guests naturally pause: welcome tables, bar menus, table cards, and near the guestbook. A brief announcement from the DJ or host often produces a stronger response than additional signs. Avoid requiring an account or app installation after the scan.

After the event

Keep the contribution link available for guests who upload on the following day, moderate the collection before broader sharing, and export originals while the event context is still fresh. Communicate a clear deadline if the album will later become read-only.
